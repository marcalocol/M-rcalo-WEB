Personal data processing policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This is an English translation provided for convenience. In case of any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

At Márcalo we take care of your personal data. This policy explains what data we collect, what we use it for and how you can exercise your rights, in accordance with Law 1581 of 2012 and Decree 1377 of 2013 (compiled in Decree 1074 of 2015).

1. Data controller

  • Name: MARCALO COL SAS
  • Domicile: Bogotá, Colombia
  • Email: contacto_@marcalocol.com
  • WhatsApp: +57 300 957 4511

2. What data we collect

  • Contact data: name, WhatsApp number, email.
  • Data about your brand and business: brand name, logo, your company's activity and the goods or services you offer.
  • Data for the registration process: name or company name of the owner, identification number (ID or tax ID), address, and any other data required by the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce.
  • Payment data: confirmation of your payment. Card or account data is processed directly by the payment platform; Márcalo does not store it.

We do not collect sensitive data or data from minors.

3. What we use your data for

  • To answer your inquiries and send you quotes.
  • To provide the services you hire: searches, advice, diagnoses and trademark registration processes.
  • To file applications and documents with the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce on your behalf, when the service includes it.
  • To keep you informed about your process.
  • To manage payments and collections and handle requests, complaints and claims.
  • To send you information about our services, only if you authorize it. You can ask us to stop at any time.

4. Who we share your data with

  • Superintendence of Industry and Commerce: when we file your application. Please note that trademark application information (such as the owner's name) is published in the authority's databases and Gazette.
  • Technology providers that help us provide the service, such as WhatsApp (messaging), the payment platform and the website hosting service. Some are located outside Colombia and may only use your data to provide that service to us.
  • Authorities, when required by law.

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

5. Your rights

As the owner of your data, you have the right to:

  • Know, update and correct your data.
  • Request proof of the authorization you gave us.
  • Know how we have used your data.
  • Revoke the authorization or request deletion of your data, when there is no legal or contractual obligation to keep it.
  • Access your data free of charge.
  • File complaints with the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce, after having submitted your inquiry or claim to us.

6. How to exercise your rights

Write to us at contacto_@marcalocol.com or on WhatsApp at +57 300 957 4511, stating your name, your identification and what you need.

  • Inquiries: we respond within 10 business days at most. If that is not possible, we will let you know and respond within 5 additional business days at most.
  • Claims (correction, update, deletion or revocation): we respond within 15 business days at most. If that is not possible, we will let you know and respond within 8 additional business days at most.

7. Authorization

By sending us your data through the website, WhatsApp or another channel, and checking the authorization box where applicable, you authorize us to process it under this policy.

8. Security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect your data against loss, misuse or unauthorized access. We keep it for as long as necessary to provide the service and meet our legal obligations.

9. Validity and changes

This policy is effective from its publication. If we change it significantly, we will announce it on this website.
