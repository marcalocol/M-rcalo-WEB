FAQ

About the Express search

Does the search tell me if I can register my trademark?

No. We send you the matches, without analysis. To know how likely your trademark is to be approved, there is the 360° Brand Diagnosis.

Does it cover other countries?

No. Only trademarks registered or pending in Colombia, before the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce.

How do I pay?

Online with Bold. If the online payment fails, we send you a payment link on WhatsApp.

Is the price deducted if I later choose a registration plan?

No. The prior-service discount applies to Let's Talk 1-on-1 and the 360° Brand Diagnosis, not to the Express search.
