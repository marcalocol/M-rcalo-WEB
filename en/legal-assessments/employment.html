Employment legal assessment for your company

Employment problems usually show up when someone leaves or when an inspection arrives. We review your company today so you are not caught by surprise.

Request on WhatsApp

The risks we find most often

  • People hired as independent contractors who in practice work as employees.
  • Overtime and surcharges that are not paid or are miscalculated.
  • Dismissals without just cause or without following due process.
  • Companies without internal work regulations or without the SG-SST in place.
  • Contracts not updated with recent changes to labor law.

When to do it

Before it happens, or as soon as it does

🛡️

To prevent

Before hiring several people, changing schedules or dismissing someone. A timely review prevents lawsuits and fines from the Ministry of Labor.

🚨

If the problem already happened

Do you already have a claim, a lawsuit or a visit from the Ministry of Labor? We review your case and tell you how to catch up and reduce the risk.
