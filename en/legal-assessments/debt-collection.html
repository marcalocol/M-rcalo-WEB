What the assessment includes

🧾

Electronic invoices

Whether they are accepted and can be used as negotiable instruments to collect in court.

✍️

Promissory notes and guarantees

Whether they are properly signed, with instruction letters, and whether the guarantees you request actually work.

📆

Collection deadlines

Debts cannot be collected forever. We check which accounts are close to the statute of limitations.

🗂️

Credit policy

How you decide whom to sell to on credit, with what limit and on what terms.

📞

Collection before going to court

How and when you contact debtors, following the rules that protect them, and how to agree on payment plans.

📊

Credit bureau reports

That you meet the requirements, such as prior notice, before reporting a customer.

The risks we find most often

  • Invoices that cannot be collected in court because they were not accepted or are missing information.
  • Credit sales without a promissory note or guarantee.
  • Debts left to expire until the statute of limitations ran out.
  • Collection calls or messages at times or frequencies the law does not allow.
  • Credit bureau reports without prior notice, which can end in a complaint against your company.

When to do it

Before it happens, or as soon as it does

🛡️

To prevent

Before you start selling on credit or increase your customers' credit limits, and when your receivables start to grow.

If the problem already happened
