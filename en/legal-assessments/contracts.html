Contracts and corporate legal assessment

We review the agreements your business stands on, with customers, suppliers, partners and collaborators, so they protect you when something goes wrong.

Request on WhatsApp

The risks we find most often

  • Contracts copied from the internet or from another country, which do not fit Colombian law or your business.
  • Important deals closed verbally or over WhatsApp, hard to prove if there is a dispute.
  • An external designer or developer who still owns the rights to what they created for you.
  • Partners without clear rules for making decisions, sharing profits or leaving the company.
  • Contracts without clauses on breach, termination or how to resolve a dispute.

When to do it

Before it happens, or as soon as it does

🛡️

To prevent

Before signing an important contract, bringing in a partner or investor, launching your online store or hiring a freelancer. Reviewing in time costs much less than solving a dispute.

🚨

If the problem already happened

A customer is not paying, a supplier breached or you disagree with a partner, and the contract does not help? We review what you can do today and how to fix your documents so it does not happen again.
