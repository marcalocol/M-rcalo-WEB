When do you need it?

  • You sell online or through social media.
  • You run promotions, discounts or contests.
  • You received a complaint, or a complaint was filed with the SIC.
  • You have no terms and conditions or warranty policy.

What you get

A written document with what we found, the risks for your company and our recommendations to fix them, explained in plain words. We agree on the scope and price with you on WhatsApp before we start.
