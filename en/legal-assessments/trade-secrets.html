FAQ

About this assessment

Is a trade secret registered like a trademark?

No. It has no registration: it is protected through contracts and measures to keep it secret. That is why doing it right is key.

Does it help if someone already used my information?

Yes. The assessment shows you your options and how to prevent it from happening again.
