Trade secrets legal assessment

Your recipe, your formula, your customer list or your way of doing things may be worth more than your brand. The law only protects them if you take measures to keep them secret.

Request on WhatsApp

The risks we find most often

  • Employees and contractors handling valuable information without having signed any confidentiality commitment.
  • Everyone in the company has access to everything, with no control.
  • Meetings with potential investors or partners without a confidentiality agreement.
  • Former employees opening a similar business using your information.
  • Being unable to prove the information was secret, because it was never treated as such.

When to do it

Before it happens, or as soon as it does

🛡️

To prevent

Before hiring key staff, sharing your recipe or code with a supplier or presenting your business to an investor.

🚨

If the problem already happened

Has someone already used your information, or did a former employee open a business similar to yours? We review the actions available to you, for example for unfair competition, and close the gaps so it does not happen again.

FAQ

About this assessment

Is a trade secret registered like a trademark?

No. It has no registration: it is protected through contracts and measures to keep it secret. That is why it must be done right from the start.

Which is better: patenting or keeping it secret?

It depends on your invention and your business. A patent protects for a limited time and requires publishing the invention; a secret lasts as long as it stays secret. We help you decide.

Does it help if someone already used my information?

Yes. We review the actions available to you and how to prevent it from happening again.
