Registration timeline

How long does trademark registration take in Colombia?

Without oppositions or corrections, the process before the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce usually takes 6 to 8 months. We explain each stage.

The stages

What happens after you file

1

Filing the application

The application is filed with the SIC with the name or logo and the classes you want to protect.

2

Formal examination

The SIC checks that the application is complete. If something is missing, it requests corrections that must be answered on time.

3

Publication in the Gazette

The application is published so anyone can learn about it.

4

Opposition period

For 30 business days after publication, third parties may oppose the registration.

5

Substantive examination

The SIC analyzes whether the trademark can be registered and whether any oppositions are justified.

6

Decision

The authority grants or refuses the registration. If refused, a review of the decision can be requested.

What can delay it

What makes the process longer

📝

Corrections

If the application has errors or missing information, the SIC requests corrections and the process stops until they are answered.

⚖️

Oppositions

If someone opposes, you must respond and the SIC must resolve the opposition. The process can exceed one year.

🔁

Appeals

If the trademark is refused and a review is requested, the process takes several more months.

🔍

A similar trademark

The most common cause of problems. That is why it pays to search your brand and get the 360° Brand Diagnosis before filing.

💡 Timelines are approximate and depend on the SIC's workload. In our plans we notify you of every update in your process.

FAQ

About registration timelines

Can I use my brand while the application is pending?

Yes. But until the SIC grants it you do not have the exclusive right, so it is best to file as soon as possible.

When am I protected?

The exclusive right arises with registration. Meanwhile, your application gives you priority over anyone who files a similar trademark after you.

How long does the registration last once granted?

10 years. It can be renewed for equal periods; renewal is requested within the 6 months before expiry.

Can the process be sped up?

What helps most is filing a well-prepared application from the start, to avoid corrections and reduce the risk of oppositions.
