What changes the price

What makes the investment go up or down

🏢

Whether you are an SME

SMEs pay a lower SIC fee. It is not only for companies: individuals can also qualify as SMEs based on their income, if they meet the authority's requirements.

🗂️

How many classes you protect

Each additional class adds an SIC fee. We help you choose only the ones you need.

⚖️

Oppositions or requested corrections

Responding to oppositions or office actions has an additional cost, except in the Shielded Plan, which includes it.

🔁

Whether you paid for a prior service

If you paid for Let's Talk 1-on-1 or the 360° Brand Diagnosis and choose a plan within 30 days, we deduct that amount.

FAQ

About registration costs

Is the SIC fee included in the plan price?

No. The fee is paid directly to the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce and is added to the plan price.

Can I pay in installments?

In the Self-Service and Team plans you pay 50% upfront and 50% at the second stage. The Shielded Plan can be paid in up to 3 monthly installments.

Is the Express search deducted from the plan?

No. The prior-service discount applies only to Let's Talk 1-on-1 and the 360° Brand Diagnosis.

What if I want to register several trademarks?

Message us for a quote, with a discount for each additional trademark.
