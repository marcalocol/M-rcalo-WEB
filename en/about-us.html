About us

Lawyers who think about your business

We are based in Bogotá and work remotely with individuals and companies, wherever they are. We want to be your right hand on legal matters.

Who we are

We are women lawyers specialized in intellectual property, commercial law and corporate law, with experience in the technology sector. We are based in Bogotá, but we rely on technology to advise you remotely, no matter where you are.

Who we work with

People who are creating their business and want to start on solid ground: protecting their brand, having clear contracts and knowing their obligations. And companies that are already running, because as they grow, hire more people or explore new business lines, new risks appear that need timely attention.

Beyond documents

Our work is not limited to reviewing or drafting documents. We start by understanding how your business works, identify its risks and propose solutions to mitigate them. And we make sure those solutions work in practice, in the day-to-day of your company, instead of staying on paper.

Technology helps; experience decides

Artificial intelligence makes many tasks easier, and we use it too to work faster. But no tool replaces the judgment of someone who knows how disputes are actually resolved: how to negotiate before reaching a judge, what matters when a case goes to court and which decisions today prevent a problem tomorrow. That experience is what we put at the service of your business.

Our goal

We don't want to do what every other law firm does. We want to communicate legal matters clearly and usefully, and to be the right hand of the people and companies we work with: strategic allies who know your business and stand by you at every stage, not just for one procedure.
