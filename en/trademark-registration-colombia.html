Why register it

With a registered trademark, your company can:

🛡️

Stop others from using it

No one else can use a name or logo identical or similar to yours for what you sell.

🤝

Let others use it, for a fee

You can license your brand to third parties without giving up ownership.

🏪

Grow through franchising

A franchise cannot exist without a registered trademark. It is what holds it together.

💼

Increase your company's value

A registered trademark can be sold, assigned and added to your company's value.

What few companies know

Is registering your company with the Chamber of Commerce enough? No.

Registering your company with the Chamber of Commerce does not protect your brand. Many companies find out too late: someone else registers an identical or similar name and, overnight, what they built is at risk. The only way to make sure only your company can use your brand is to register it with the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce.
🌎 And one key point: trademarks are registered country by country. If you register your brand in Colombia, it is protected in Colombia. If you sell or plan to sell in other countries, you need to register it there too.

The process

From name to certificate, step by step

1

Search your brand

Check whether an identical or similar trademark already exists in Colombia with the Express search.

2

Find out if you can register it

The 360° Brand Diagnosis tells you how likely approval is and what to protect.

3

File the application

It is filed with the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce, in the classes of goods or services you choose.

4

Publication and oppositions

The application is published in the Gazette and third parties may oppose it within the legal term.

5

SIC decision

The authority examines the trademark and grants or refuses it.

6

Registered trademark

You receive the certificate. Registration lasts 10 years and can be renewed.

FAQ

About trademark registration in Colombia

Who can register a trademark in Colombia?

Any individual or company, Colombian or foreign. The trademark is registered in the name of the applicant.

What can I register?

Your brand name, your logo or both. In the 360° Brand Diagnosis we recommend what to protect for your case.

What are classes of goods or services?

Trademarks are registered for specific goods or services, grouped into classes (the Nice Classification). You pay a fee for each class you want to protect.

Do you guarantee my trademark will be approved?

No one can guarantee it: the decision belongs to the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce. What we do is study your case so your application has the best chance.

How long does registration last?

10 years from the date the SIC grants it. It can be renewed for equal periods.
