What you can register

Not just names and logos: these are the types of trademarks

In Colombia you can register as a trademark any sign that distinguishes your goods or services. The first three are the most common; we help you choose the one that best protects your business.

Word mark

Only words, letters or numbers, with no design. For example, your brand name.

Figurative mark

Only an image or logo, with no words.

Combined mark

Words and design together: your brand name with its logo.

Three-dimensional

The shape of your product or its packaging.

Combined three-dimensional

A three-dimensional shape together with words or a design.

Motion mark

A sequence of moving images, such as your logo animation.

Color mark

A color or combination of colors that identifies your product.

Sound mark

A sound or melody your customers associate with you.

Olfactory mark

A scent that identifies your product.

Gesture mark

A gesture or body movement.

Tactile mark

A texture or feel to the touch.

Position mark

An element always placed in the same spot on your product.

💡 Non-traditional trademarks (three-dimensional, motion, color, sound, olfactory, gesture, tactile and position marks) have special requirements. We help you assess whether your case qualifies.

What few companies know

Is registering your company with the Chamber of Commerce enough? No.

Registering your company with the Chamber of Commerce does not protect your brand. Many companies find out too late: someone else registers an identical or similar name and, overnight, what they built is at risk. The only way to make sure only your company can use your brand is to register it with the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce.
🌎 And one key point: trademarks are registered country by country. If you register your brand in Colombia, it is protected in Colombia. If you sell or plan to sell in other countries, you need to register it there too. What about international registration? We explain it in the FAQ →

FAQ

About trademark registration in Colombia

Who can register a trademark in Colombia?

Any individual or company, Colombian or foreign. The trademark is registered in the name of the applicant.

What can I register?

Your brand name, your logo or both, but also shapes, colors, sounds and other signs. See the types of trademarks. In the 360° Brand Diagnosis we recommend what to protect for your case.

What are classes of goods or services?

Trademarks are registered for specific goods or services, grouped into classes (the Nice Classification). You pay a fee for each class you want to protect.

Can I protect my trademark in other countries? Is there an international trademark registration?

Trademarks are protected country by country: registering in Colombia protects your trademark only in Colombia. There is the Madrid System (Madrid Protocol), which Colombia is part of. It allows anyone who already has a base application or registration in Colombia to file a single application from here to protect their trademark in other member countries. Each country decides whether to protect it. It is an alternative, but not always the most recommended path: sometimes filing directly in each country makes more sense. It all depends on your case: where you sell or plan to sell, how solid your trademark is in Colombia and how much you want to invest. Message us and we will review it with you.

Do you guarantee my trademark will be approved?

No one can guarantee it: the decision belongs to the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce. What we do is study your case so your application has the best chance.

How long does registration last?

10 years from the date the SIC grants it. It can be renewed for equal periods.
