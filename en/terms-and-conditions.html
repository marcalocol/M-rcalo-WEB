1. Who we are

Márcalo is a trademark registration and protection advisory service in Colombia, provided by MARCALO COL SAS, domiciled in Bogotá, Colombia.

Contact: WhatsApp +57 300 957 4511 · Email contacto_@marcalocol.com.

2. Our services

The scope, price and payment method of each service are those published on marcalocol.com at the time of hiring, or those we send you in a written proposal.

  • Express search: a list of registered or pending trademarks that resemble yours in spelling and sound. It is only a list of matches: it does not include analysis or an opinion on whether your trademark can be registered. It covers only trademarks in Colombia, registered or pending before the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce, and is paid online through Bold.
  • Let's Talk 1-on-1: a one-hour virtual advisory session.
  • 360° Brand Diagnosis: a written document analyzing prior marks, competitors, likelihood of registration and the recommended strategy.
  • Registration plans (Self-Service, Team and Shielded): support in the trademark registration process before the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce, according to what each plan includes.
  • Legal assessments (contracts, trade secrets, employment, debt collection and consumer protection): a written review of your company's situation in the chosen area, with recommendations. Scope and price are agreed on WhatsApp before we start.

3. What we cannot guarantee

The decision to grant or refuse a trademark registration belongs to the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce. We put all our knowledge and care into giving your application the best chance, but we cannot guarantee that the trademark will be approved, nor control the authority's timelines or oppositions filed by third parties.

Our analyses are based on the information available in public databases at the time they are made, which may change or be incomplete.

4. Prices, fees and payments

  • Published prices are Márcalo's fees, in Colombian pesos.
  • The Superintendence of Industry and Commerce's official fee is not included in our prices. You pay it directly to the authority, which sets it every year.
  • The payment terms of each plan are those published on the site. In plans with staged payments, the second payment must be made at the indicated time to continue the service.
  • We start working once we receive the initial payment and the necessary information.

If you paid for Let's Talk 1-on-1 or the 360° Brand Diagnosis (or both) and hire a registration plan within 30 calendar days of payment, we deduct that amount from the plan price. This discount does not apply to the Express search, cannot be combined with other promotions and applies only to the same trademark.
